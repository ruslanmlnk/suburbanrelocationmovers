Editable moving-company theme with stable home-section settings, Services and Locations custom post types, quote requests and starter content.

== Installation ==
Upload and activate the theme, then open Appearance > Starter Content and run the importer.

== Editing ==
Edit the full home page under Appearance > Home Sections. Add service articles under Services and local SEO articles under Locations. Update company-wide contact details under Appearance > Company Details.
